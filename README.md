# Twilight Technology Site — Public Product & Guided-Intake Platform

> **Technical equivalent:** Twilight Technology's customer-facing product catalog, live AI product demonstration, secure lead-capture workflow, and business acquisition surface.

The production website for [Twilight Technology](https://twilighttech.io), presenting the company's products and services through a clear **Enter, Sustain, Further** technology framework.

## Role in the Twilight ecosystem

- **Product catalog** — Explains managed IT, AI, security, guides, community, and customer-facing products.
- **Live product demonstration** — Shows the Website Front Desk experience instead of merely describing it.
- **Guided intake** — Routes visitors through approved, page-aware answers and structured next steps.
- **Secure lead capture** — Uses edge validation and server-side handoff into the business records and notification workflow.
- **Human handoff** — Keeps customer communication bounded, predictable, and connected to Twilight Technology's real operations.
