// What it does

Useful before it is clever

The goal is not endless conversation. It is getting a visitor to the right next step with less friction.

01 / Route

Guides the visitor

Clear buttons route people toward services, FAQs, resources, booking, community, or a real person.

02 / Capture

Collects a clean lead

Asks only the questions you approve, keeps sensitive details out, and delivers the summary to your inbox.

03 / Protect

Blocks the foolishness

Turnstile verification, input limits, honeypot protection, rate limiting, and server-side validation are built in.

04 / Match

Looks like your business

Your colors, tone, assistant name, offers, links, and language. It should feel native to the site, not stapled on.

05 / Adapt

Understands the page

The front desk can prioritize different routes on Services, About, pricing, resource, or campaign pages.

06 / Measure

Shows what people need

Privacy-conscious events show which routes visitors choose and where interest turns into an inquiry.
