# Twilight Tech Solutions launch

## Campaign

- Public offer: **Twilight Tech Solutions**
- Canonical landing page: https://twilighttech.io/ai/yours/
- Primary audience: owner-led businesses, small teams, operations leaders, and technical founders whose work is fragmented across tools and tribal knowledge
- Core promise: Twilight Tech designs a purpose-built software environment around how the operation actually works
- Primary conversion: one high-level operating problem submitted through the existing secure Front Desk lead lane
- Campaign creative: images/og-solutions.png
- Proof path: landing page -> featured system -> Twilight Tech Labs or J-Bot Operations -> bring a problem

## Positioning

### Category

Purpose-built operational systems.

### Headline

Your operation should not live in six tabs and one person's head.

### One-sentence offer

Show us how the work actually moves. Twilight Tech designs the software environment around it—bringing data, workflows, AI, controls, and human decisions into one place.

### Differentiator

The offer is not a generic chatbot, dashboard, automation package, or software resale. It begins with the real operating loop and models the system around its people, state, decisions, risks, approvals, and evidence.

## Proof hierarchy

1. **Pulse War Room** — research, decision, risk, governance, and paper-execution environment.
2. **BrightPath Academy** — curriculum, learner memory, adaptive study, grading, journals, quests, and progress presented as a learning world.
3. **J-Bot Command Systems** — governed, local-first business coordination with approvals and receipts.

Every proof item states its current boundary. Replacement-value estimates and trading-performance claims are intentionally excluded from the ad page.

## Recommended first-day setup

- Start with the founder-style primary post as an organic LinkedIn post.
- Use Variant A as the first paid creative because the pain is recognizable before the audience understands the category.
- Use Variant B as the retargeting message for landing-page visitors who did not submit.
- Keep the call to action as **Bring us the messy part**.
- Optimize for landing-page views first; switch to lead optimization only after the new Solutions Problem Submitted event has enough volume to train against.
