The job Rover was built for

Good support breaks when the answer, the access, and the handoff stop lining up.

A requester should not have to search five places, guess which instructions are current, or repeat the whole story after escalation.

Rover gives the first response a disciplined route: use reviewed knowledge when it fits, preserve the evidence behind the answer, and hand the case to a technician when judgment or authority is required.

Built from the work. Rover carries practical lessons from Tier 1 support through systems administration into the way answers are found, access is respected, and escalations are prepared.

One request // one accountable route

Rover handles the first response and prepares the next one.

Control is part of the product

Rover keeps the work inside its lane.

01

Reviewed knowledge drives the released path. Rover v1 runs on deterministic policy and approved sources without requiring a live model provider.

02

Action scope stays explicit. Rover's ticket writes are narrowly defined; acceptance left accounts, permissions, workstations, and ticket records unchanged.

03

Access follows the role. Requesters stay in Self-Service, technicians receive the tools for their queue, and anonymous access is denied.

04

Technicians stay in control. Rover guides, records, and prepares the handoff while people own approval and consequential action.
