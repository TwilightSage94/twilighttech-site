// The evidence model

A clear record of how you worked.

A finished exercise should show someone how you think—how you checked the facts before making a change, respected an access boundary, and left a handoff note another technician could pick up cold.

Deliberate boundary

Enter uses simulated, bounded environments. It does not provide production access, impersonate real employers, guarantee certification or employment, or convert private Labs projects into one merged training product.

01 // ORIENT

What did you notice?

Check the account, device, symptom, scope, and missing context before making a change.

02 // DECIDE

Why this next step?

Tie your action to a real finding and note which shortcuts or risky changes you ruled out.

03 // DOCUMENT

What survives the exercise?

Leave a ticket note, timeline, checklist, or escalation handoff that stands on its own.

04 // EXPLAIN

Can another person follow it?

Summarize the impact, open questions, and next owner in plain English.
