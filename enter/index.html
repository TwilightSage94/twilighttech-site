// The evidence model

Not just completion. A record of judgment.

A finished scenario should make your thinking inspectable. The useful proof is not a decorative score. It is the trail showing how you oriented yourself, handled uncertainty, respected the boundary, and communicated the next step.

Deliberate boundary

Enter uses simulated, bounded environments. It does not provide production access, impersonate real employers, guarantee certification or employment, or convert private Labs projects into one merged training product.

01 // ORIENT

What did you notice?

Identify the people, system, symptom, scope, and missing context before acting.

02 // DECIDE

Why this next step?

Connect the observation to a bounded action and explain the alternative you rejected.

03 // DOCUMENT

What survives the exercise?

Leave a ticket note, timeline, checklist, handoff, finding, or other work-shaped artifact.

04 // EXPLAIN

Can another person follow it?

Translate the technical path into a clear account of impact, uncertainty, and next action.
